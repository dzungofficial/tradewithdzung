Learn Trading With A Complete System

Taught by Dzung, an XAUUSD and Forex trader and MQL5 EA developer. Every lesson is based on real trading experience, not theory alone. By the end of this program, you will be able to analyze the market, enter trades with discipline, and build a sustainable trading process.

Level 01

BEGINNER LEVEL

Goal: Understand how the market works and avoid losing money through preventable mistakes.

01

Week 1 — Market Fundamentals

Build a foundation in Forex, Gold, charts, and MetaTrader 5.

  • What is Forex? How do financial markets work?
  • Currency pairs explained, and why trade Gold (XAUUSD)?
  • How to open a demo account with PU Prime or IC Markets
  • MetaTrader 5 — reading charts and placing orders
  • Candlesticks — types and what they mean
  • Timeframes from M1 to MN — meaning and when to use them
02

Week 2 — Risk Management First

Risk management protects your account and builds consistency.

  • Risk management — why it matters
  • Lot size explained with real numerical examples
  • The 0.5% risk rule for every trade
  • Stop Loss and Take Profit — always use both
  • The maximum 2% daily loss rule
  • Beginner psychology — FOMO, revenge trading, and overtrading
WEEK 2 ASSIGNMENT
Demo Practice
Task Trade on a demo account for two weeks
Requirement Complete 10 trade reports
Include Entry · SL · TP · Results
Level 02

INTERMEDIATE LEVEL

Goal: Read charts confidently and identify setups independently.

03

Week 3 — Technical Analysis

  • Support and Resistance — identifying real and false levels
  • Trend identification — higher highs and lower lows
  • Market structure — uptrend, downtrend, and range
  • EMA (20, 50, 200) — using them for confirmation
  • ATR — measuring volatility and setting accurate SL levels
  • Sessions — London, New York, and Asia — how XAUUSD behaves in each
04

Week 4 — SMC Fundamentals

  • Who is Smart Money and what do they do?
  • Liquidity — stop hunts and taking highs and lows
  • Order Blocks — how to identify them on a chart
  • Break of Structure (BOS) versus Change of Character (CHoCH)
  • Fair Value Gaps (FVG) — what they are and how to trade them
  • Confluence — combining two or three factors before entering
WEEK 4 ASSIGNMENT
Backtesting
Task Mark up the charts from the previous week
Target Identify at least five valid setups
Setup BOS / CHoCH with Order Block confluence

PROFESSIONAL LEVEL

05

Weeks 5–6 — Advanced SMC and Precise Execution

  • Traps and false moves — how Smart Money misleads retail traders
  • News trading — how to use it or avoid it
  • Prop firm challenges — rules, strategy, and how to pass

  • Account management — handling client capital responsibly
